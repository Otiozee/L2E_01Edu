# My Fairness Contract

## I will use AI when:

- I’ve tried the problem myself for at least 20 minutes or until I’m really stuck.
- I want to understand why my solution is slow/wrong.
- I’m curious about other ways to solve it after I have one working version.

## I will NOT use AI when:

- It’s a timed test or interview.
- It’s a school assignment that’s supposed to test me.
- I’m learning the absolute basics.

## I know I’m using AI fairly when:

- I can close the chat and still explain the code line by line.
- I can solve a similar but not identical problem the next day without help.
- I feel like I actually got smarter, not just finished quicker.

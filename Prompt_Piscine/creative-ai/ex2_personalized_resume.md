# Exercise 2: Personalized Resume Summary

## Prompt 1: Software Engineer in Nigeria
"Create a 3-sentence professional summary for a Nigerian software engineer skilled in Python, cloud computing, and AI."

### Model response
```txt
A highly skilled Nigerian software engineer with expertise in Python, cloud computing, and AI. Experienced in developing scalable solutions for fintech and e-commerce platforms. Passionate about using technology to solve local challenges and drive innovation in Africa.
```
## Prompt 2: Marketing Specialist in Nigeria
"Create a 3-sentence professional summary for a Nigerian marketing specialist skilled in social media, brand strategy, and event management."

### Model response
```txt
A results-oriented Nigerian marketing specialist with expertise in social media, brand strategy, and event management. Proven track record of increasing brand awareness and engagement for local businesses. Enthusiastic about leveraging digital platforms to connect with Nigeria’s diverse audiences.
```

## Reflection 
Summaries adapt to Nigerian professional contexts. Technical or marketing skills are clearly highlighted, and outputs reflect local work environments and industries.

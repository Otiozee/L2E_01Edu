# Exercise 1: Generating a Short Story from a Theme

## Prompt 1: Friendship Theme in Lagos
"Write a short story about friendship in Lagos in under 300 words."

### Model response
```txt
Tola and Chike had been friends since childhood in Lagos Island. Every weekend, they raced their bicycles along the Lekki waterfront, laughing as the waves splashed nearby. One day, Chike’s family announced they were moving to Abuja and Tola felt her heart break. They promised to stay in touch through phone calls and video chats. Years later, they met again at the National Theatre, and it felt as if no time had passed. True friendship, they realized, survives any distance.
```

## Prompt 2: Space Exploration Theme from Nigeria
"Write a short story about a Nigerian astronaut exploring the Moon in under 300 words."

### Model response
```txt
Captain Adaora was the first Nigerian astronaut to land on the Moon. As she gazed at Earth from the lunar surface, the blue and green orb reminded her of home. Her team collected samples and conducted experiments while recording videos for Nigerian students watching live. Adaora’s diary ended with a reflection: “No matter how far we travel, our roots remind us where we come from. Nigeria travels with me.”
```

## Reflection
Both stories are culturally contextualized. The Lagos friendship story feels local and relatable, while the space exploration story highlights national pride. Word limits are respected, and outputs are coherent and engaging.

# Exercise 1: Logic Puzzle with Explanation

## Logic puzzle:
Esa is two times stronger than Attah, Bob is strong, but not as much as Attah. Who is the strongest?

## Prompt:
Explain your reasoning before giving the final answer.

## Model Response:

### Reasoning:
```markdown
- Esa is stated to be two times stronger than Attah. This directly means Esa’s strength is greater than Attah’s (specifically, if Attah has strength level S, then Esa has strength level 2S).
- Bob is described as “strong, but not as much as Attah.” This explicitly places Bob below Attah in strength (Bob < Attah, or Bob < S).
- Therefore, the order of strength from strongest to weakest is: Esa (2S) > Attah (S) > Bob (< S).
```
### Final answer:
```markdown
Esa is the strongest.
```
Yes, the model provided step by step reasoning before stating the answer.

### Analysis:
The reasoning follows a clear comparative chain: first, Esa is defined as having twice Attah’s strength (Esa = 2S while Attah = S), establishing Esa as stronger than Attah; secondly, Bob is stated to be less strong than Attah (Bob < S), placing him below Attah; combining these two comparisons gave the final ordered ranking from strongest to weakest: Esa > Attah > Bob.

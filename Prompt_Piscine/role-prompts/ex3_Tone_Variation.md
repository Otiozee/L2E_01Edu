# Exercise 3: Tone Variation with the Same Task

## Task: Write an email inviting a candidate to a technical interview for a Software Engineer position.

### Friendly Tone

Prompt:

Write an email inviting a candidate to a technical interview for a Software Engineer position. Use a warm, friendly, and conversational tone. Include the interview date (March 10 at 2:00 PM), mention that it will be conducted via Zoom, explain that the interview will include a live coding exercise, and ask the candidate to confirm their availability. Keep it professional but approachable.

Output:

```markdown
Subject: Excited to chat next week, Alex!
Hi Alex,
Super happy to let you know — you’re invited to the next step for the Software Engineer role at TechGrow!
We’d love to have you join us for a technical interview on Thursday, March 6 at 2:00 PM WAT. It’ll be a relaxed 90-minute video call on Google Meet (I’ll send the link tomorrow afternoon so you have it ready).
You’ll be chatting with Sarah Okoro (our Senior Engineering Manager). Expect to talk a bit about your recent project and do a short live coding session (~45 min). No pressure at all — just come as you are and show us how you think.
Looking forward to meeting you! Feel free to reply if you have any questions.
Talk soon,
[Your Name]
Talent Team @ TechGrow Solutions
```
### Professional Tone

Prompt:

Write an email inviting a candidate to a technical interview for a Software Engineer position. Use a formal and professional tone. Include the interview date (March 10 at 2:00 PM), state that it will be conducted via Zoom, note that the interview will include a live coding exercise, and request confirmation of availability. Keep the message clear and concise.

Output:
```markdown
Subject: Invitation to Technical Interview – Software Engineer Position
Dear Alex Johnson,
I am pleased to inform you that you have been selected to proceed to the technical interview stage for the Software Engineer position at TechGrow Solutions.
The interview is scheduled as follows:

Date: Thursday, 6 March 2025
Time: 14:00 – 15:30 WAT
Format: Video interview via Google Meet
Interviewer: Sarah Okoro, Senior Engineering Manager

A meeting link will be sent to you 24 hours prior to the scheduled time. The session will include a discussion of your recent project experience and a 45-minute live coding exercise.
Please confirm your availability by replying to this email at your earliest convenience. Should you have any questions in the meantime, do not hesitate to contact me.
We look forward to speaking with you.
Best regards,
[Your Name]
Recruiter
TechGrow Solutions
[Email] | [Phone]
```

### Comparing the output:
The tone changes the personality of the message, not the information. Both communicate the same details but create a different impression for the candidate.

- The friendly version builds rapport and enthusiasm.

- The professional version emphasizes clarity, structure, and formality.

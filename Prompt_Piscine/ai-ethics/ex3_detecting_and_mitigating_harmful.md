## Part C – Reflection
- If AI hallucinates wrong info and I don't catch it, people will get hurt (bad advice, fake news).
- In apps, there should be human review, disclaimers everywhere, rate limits on sensitive topics.
- The flaw if rely on AI to check itself is that it can be overconfident or biased too.
- Essential human skills needed includes: judgment, skepticism, and knowing when to doubt output.
